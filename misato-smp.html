Y=0 is the line.

It's just survival Minecraft, but with one big difference: Y=0 separates safety from PvP.

Top to bottom, the way the world is laid out:

Above the line

PvP is on. Bases can be raided, players can fight, and if you want to keep your territory you'll have to defend it.

Y = 40

No digging out of a fight

When you're in combat you get a 30-second tag. While it's active, blocks below Y=40 can't be broken, so you can't just dig straight down and disappear.

The server

Misato SMP is a vanilla survival server. You mine, build, farm and explore like you normally would. What changes is how you deal with other players, and that's what the line at Y=0 is for.

It runs on a dedicated machine instead of a cheap shared host: an Intel i7 (9th gen), 32GB of DDR4, a 512GB SSD, Linux and Fabric. The goal is to keep the game running smoothly. It won't promise zero lag, because nobody honest can, but it isn't sharing a CPU with a pile of other servers.

The full breakdown is in Under the Hood.

No paid advantages.

There are no paid ranks or purchases that give players an advantage in survival or PvP.

Rules

Short list. Most of it comes down to the line at Y=0.

PvP

Y > 0PvP enabled

Y ≤ 0PvP disabled

Combat

Combat puts a 30-second tag on you.

Escape

While you're tagged, you can't mine below Y=40. Digging away from a fight won't work.

Cheating

Don't cheat. Mods that only change how the game runs or looks are fine. Anything that gives you an edge over other players isn't. The full list is under Mods.

Exploits

Don't use exploits to break combat fairness.

Mods

Server side is Fabric plus a handful of performance and utility mods, listed in Under the Hood. On your side the line is simple: mods that help the game run or look better are fine, and anything that gives you an unfair advantage isn't.

Allowed

  • SodiumFaster rendering.
  • IrisShader support.
  • ShadersShader packs, if you want the game to look better.
  • Mini-mapsA map in the corner of your screen.

Not allowed

  • X-raySeeing ores through the terrain.
  • Fly hacksFlying in survival.
  • Kill AuraAuto-attacking players near you.
  • Speed hacksMoving faster than the game lets you.
  • Other cheatsIf it gives you an edge, it's a cheat.

The more technical version
Fabric
A lightweight mod loader. The server and the mods listed here are built for it.
Lithium
Optimizes parts of the game's tick logic, like mob AI, block updates and entity movement, while aiming to keep vanilla behaviour.
FerriteCore
Reduces memory usage by sharing duplicated data, mostly block state data.
EasyAuth
Adds registration and login so accounts can be protected with a password.
FTB Essentials
A utility command mod covering the usual server commands, such as teleport requests, homes and warps.
Chunky
Pre-generates chunks in a radius around a point, so the server isn't generating new terrain while players explore that area.
